About Us

Vic Green Happy Home Foundation is a healthcare-focused non-governmental organization dedicated to supporting individuals and communities through improved access to healthcare, health education, and compassionate assistance.

Our goal is to contribute to healthier and happier communities by helping people access the healthcare support and information they need.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote better health and well-being by:

* Supporting people who need healthcare assistance
* Promoting health awareness and education
* Encouraging access to essential healthcare services
* Supporting vulnerable members of our communities
* Organizing community-focused healthcare initiatives

Our Vision

We envision communities where everyone has the opportunity to access essential healthcare, receive reliable health information, and live a healthier and more dignified life.

Our Services

Our activities may include:

* 🏥 Healthcare Support — Helping individuals and families access healthcare assistance.
* ❤️ Community Outreach — Organizing initiatives that support people within local communities.
* 📚 Health Education — Promoting awareness about healthy living and healthcare.
* 🤝 Community Support — Supporting vulnerable individuals and families.
* 📋 Registration & Volunteer Programs — Providing opportunities for people to register their interest in our programs and get involved.
